FAQ

Service Questions

Quick answers about our services.

Can I combine multiple services in one order?
Absolutely! You can mix wash & fold, dry cleaning, ironing, and bedding in a single pickup. Just let us know what you need and we'll sort everything accordingly.
Do you use my detergent or yours?
We use our own premium, skin-friendly detergents. However, if you have a preferred detergent or need hypoallergenic products, just let us know and we'll accommodate your request.
What happens if my clothes are damaged?
In the rare event of damage, we take full responsibility. Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos, and we'll compensate you fairly per our damage policy.
Is there a minimum order for pickup?
There's no strict minimum order. However, for areas further from our facility, we may require a minimum order value to cover delivery costs. Contact us to confirm for your location.
