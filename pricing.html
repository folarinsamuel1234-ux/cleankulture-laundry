Pricing FAQ

Common Pricing Questions

Are there any hidden fees?
Absolutely not. The prices you see are what you pay. The only additional fee is pickup & delivery (which is free on your first order and waived for orders above ₦10,000).
Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?
Yes! We offer volume discounts for commercial clients and regular customers. Contact us via WhatsApp or our contact page for a custom quote.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, cash on delivery, and mobile payments via Opay, Palmpay, and other popular Nigerian payment apps.
How does the express service pricing work?
Express service adds ₦1,000 to your total order for same-day delivery (orders must be placed before 10am). Next-day priority adds ₦500.
