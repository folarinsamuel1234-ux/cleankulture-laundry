FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your first order.

How long does the service take?
Standard service is completed within 48 hours. We also offer same-day express service for urgent orders — simply let us know when booking.
Do you offer same-day delivery?
Yes! Same-day service is available for orders placed before 10am. An additional express fee applies. Contact us via WhatsApp to confirm availability in your area.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, cash on delivery, and payments via Opay, Palmpay and other popular mobile payment apps.
How do I schedule a pickup?
You can schedule via WhatsApp, by calling us directly, or by filling the booking form on this website. We'll confirm your pickup time within minutes.
What fabrics do you clean?
We clean cotton, linen, wool, polyester, silk, denim, and most synthetic fabrics. Delicate items like silk and wool are handled with specialist dry-cleaning methods.
Can I track my order?
Yes! We send you WhatsApp updates at every stage — pickup confirmed, washing in progress, and out for delivery. Order tracking is coming soon on our app.
